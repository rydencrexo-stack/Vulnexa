Task 01 · Conceptual Overview

The Role of Reconnaissance in Cybersecurity

Reconnaissance is the initial phase of any security assessment. Before an adversary or an automated defense platform can find vulnerabilities, they must establish an exhaustive inventory of reachable assets.

Modern organizations frequently deploy staging environments, forgotten cloud storage buckets, and microservice subdomains that remain unmonitored.

Core Philosophy

You cannot defend what you do not know exists. Attack Surface Management continuously indexes internet-facing assets to eliminate shadow IT.

Task 02 · Passive OSINT

Certificate Transparency Logs (CT Logs)

Whenever an organization issues a TLS/SSL certificate from a public Certificate Authority (e.g. Let's Encrypt, DigiCert), the certificate must be published to a publicly auditable Certificate Transparency (CT) Log.

Recon engines monitor CT log streams in real-time to discover newly provisioned subdomains within seconds of certificate creation.

Why are Certificate Transparency logs a goldmine for passive reconnaissance?
They publicly record every SSL certificate issued for root domains and subdomains.
They contain plain-text database passwords.
They bypass web application firewall IP rate limits.
